# Minimal public federal facts and original analysis

Source: U.S. Department of Transportation, Bureau of Transportation Statistics, TranStats Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance (1987-present). The package uses public federal statistical facts with attribution. Original code is MIT licensed; the code license does not relicense third-party expressive material.

Publish factual day/destination/carrier sufficient counts, source schema and capture hashes, original analysis and reviewed independent figures/manuscript. Keep the full archive, full CSV, tail numbers and flight-level identifiers private. Do not copy source manuals, logos, third-party reports, individual contact data or source narratives. No European data values are reproduced or transformed, and no permission to modify a European dataset is inferred.

The recorded source is not a historical first-publication snapshot. Source values and reporting populations can be revised. This is independent analysis, not DOT/BTS endorsement or official reporting. No carrier safety, person, employment or vendor-eligibility decisions.

Official metadata: [download form](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/DL_SelectFields.aspx?QO_fu146_anzr=b0-gvzr&gnoyr_VQ=FGJ) and [table definitions](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/TableInfo.asp?QO_fu146_anzr=b0-gvzr&gnoyr_VQ=FGJ). Exact capture hashes and resulting archive URL will be retained. Separate formal-publication content, Azure optimization, credentials and excluded records remain private.
